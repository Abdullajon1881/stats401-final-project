    Within ten minutes of both healthcare and education

    The intersection of the healthcare and education results, read as committed by the analysis: residents whose population cell reaches both. It is not the sum of the two shares and not a score.

    Where the routing sources are

    Points are routing-source proxies: the destinations the network routing starts from. Healthcare and education points sit at the committed representative coordinates of each mapped facility, and district outlines are the current analysis geography. Hollow points lie outside the analysis districts but were kept as routing sources, because they can still serve population cells inside the study area. The percentages come from pedestrian-network routing over population cells, not from point density or drawn buffers. OpenStreetMap coverage may be incomplete, and a point is not a measure of a facility’s capacity or service quality.

    Reading the numbers

    These numbers are not supposed to match. Both describe a modelled ten-minute walk along the same pedestrian network, but they are built to answer different questions.

    Three differences between the two methods
    Difference Current snapshot Standardized time series
    Metro source Entrance-aware: mapped metro entrances, with the station point used only where no entrance is mapped Station-centre proxy: today’s station-centre coordinates, fixed for every year, with no entrances
    Population Current population surface, SIAT-calibrated to official district totals Annual WorldPop model weights, not SIAT-calibrated
    Analytical objective A current-period accessibility estimate A controlled year-to-year comparison, with the pedestrian network and district geography held fixed

    The current number answers what does access look like now? The standardized number answers how did access change under one comparable analytical frame?

    They are not interchangeable, and the gap between them is a difference of method, not a change in access or an error in either. The standardized series holds today’s streets and station positions fixed; it is not a reconstruction of historical streets or entrances.

    Standardized comparison

    The time series holds today’s pedestrian network, station-centre coordinates and district geography fixed, then changes only the station opening state and the annual WorldPop model weights. Its values are comparable with one another, not with the current snapshot above.
