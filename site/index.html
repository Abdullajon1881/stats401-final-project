Method and uncertainty

What is measured

Population is modelled at roughly 100 m cells, calibrated district by district to official — totals, then classified by walking distance along the OpenStreetMap pedestrian network to the nearest metro access point and to the nearest mapped bus stop.

What is not measured

Physical walking access only. Nothing here reflects how often a service runs, how long it operates, transfers, in-vehicle time, reliability, crowding, fare, or whether a route goes anywhere useful.

Known limits

The link from a cell centre to the network is a straight line to the nearest mapped walkable edge and may not be physically walkable, so its effect on individual cells is uncertain. Within-district population weights come from WorldPop R2025A, an alpha product of unverified within-district accuracy. Bus stops are OpenStreetMap only.
