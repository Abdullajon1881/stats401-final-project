Motivation & question

A metro line on a map does not mean residents can reach it on foot. Rail corridors, canals and walled blocks make two points the same crow-flies distance a very different walk apart.

How much of Tashkent's analysed population is within a modelled ten-minute walk of metro access, and where does the bus network fill the gap?

Five questions: (1) citywide metro share; (2) district variation; (3) bus-only dependence; (4) density vs access; (5) bazaar–transit overlap.

The ten-minute model

  • 10 minutes at 4.8 km/h = 800 m of distance along the OpenStreetMap pedestrian network — not a straight-line circle.
  • Each population cell is routed to the nearest metro access point and bus stop; metro routing is entrance-aware (154 access points: 144 entrances, 10 station-point fallbacks).
  • Three mutually exclusive classes that sum to 100%: metro / bus-only / underserved.

Data & method

  • OpenStreetMap (ODbL): pedestrian network, stations, entrances, bus stops, bazaars, boundaries.
  • SIAT 2026-Q2: official district population totals.
  • WorldPop R2025A: distributes each district's official total within its boundary only.
  • 12 matched districts; 3,212,200 residents. Yangi Toshkent is excluded — no official total to calibrate against.
  • Python geospatial analysis; static D3 + MapLibre dashboard.

What the model does not claim

  • Physical walking proximity, not observed trips, frequency, fares, transfers or service quality.
  • Cell-to-network connectors are straight lines that may cross unmapped barriers.
  • Headline moves with walking speed: 10.0% at 4.0 km/h → 17.3% at 5.6 km/h.
  • Mapped bazaars/facilities are an OSM lower bound, not a census of capacity or quality.

Everyday destinations

Same 800 m model: 46.9% reach mapped healthcare, 72.5% education, 14.4% a bazaar. The committed healthcare-and-education intersection is 43.7% (residents reaching both, not a sum).

Takeaway

On this model, metro walking access in Tashkent is narrow and concentrated along the lines — about one analysed resident in seven. The large majority can walk to a bus stop but not the metro, and that dependence is very unevenly spread across districts. Everything is reproducible from audited outputs, with the uncertainty on the page rather than buried.
